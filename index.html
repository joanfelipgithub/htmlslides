📥 Bulk Import Pins or Quiz Questions

Paste copied lines directly from Excel (tab-separated). Existing pins are updated by Slide + Pin #; new numbers create new pins. Slides that don't exist yet are created as blank placeholders.

Format 1 – Quiz question (short):
Slide | MC or TF | Question_Text | Opt_A | Opt_B | Opt_C | Opt_D | Correct_Answer
Format 2 – "Copy Table" output (round-trip):
Slide | [Quiz] | Text | Type | Correct | – | – | – | – | – | Opt_A | Opt_B | Opt_C | Opt_D
Slide | Pin# | Tooltip | – | – | Outline HEX | BG HEX | X % | Y % | Rotation | – | – | – | – | Pin_ID
Both formats can be mixed. Pins are matched by Pin_ID (last column), so changing a pin number never creates a duplicate. Without a Pin_ID column, rows are matched by position when a slide has the same number of rows as pins, otherwise by pin number. A header row is ignored. Use "-" or leave a cell empty to keep the current value.
